## Scope

ALM (Atomic Logical Method) is presented as a research programme for examining structural relationships among mathematical models through explicitly stated assumptions, domains, invariants, transformations, and proof obligations. Formal analogies, finite computational checks, and elementary identities do not establish a general transfer theorem.

## Scientific status

- **3D Navier–Stokes:** regularity and conditional estimates are research targets; a general existence-and-smoothness proof is **not** claimed.
- **Riemann Hypothesis:** analytical and spectral approaches are exploratory; **no proof** is claimed.
- **Hodge Conjecture:** geometric and cohomological connections are exploratory; **no proof** is claimed.
- **Elementary examples:** bounded, classical results (involution projectors; Lipschitz error transport; spectral counterexample) are used to illustrate an evidence standard, not as original proofs of open conjectures.

## Open proof obligations

Any proposed bridge among the problems would require (1) precise mathematical domains and a non-circular statement; (2) explicit, well-defined maps; (3) proofs of which invariants survive those maps; (4) analysis of information loss and reconstruction; (5) compatibility under composition; and (6) a substantive theorem in each original setting rather than merely in an analogy or finite model.

All such **general transfer obligations remain open** in this public edition. Evidence must be labelled *proved in the stated domain*, *checked in a specified model*, *conditional*, *conjectural*, or *not evaluated*.
