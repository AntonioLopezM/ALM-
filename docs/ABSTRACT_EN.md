# ALM — Public research abstract

**Research proposer:** Antonio López Martínez.  
**Public edition:** 10 November 2026.

ALM (Atomic Logical Method) is a proposed research framework for examining structural relationships between mathematical models. It emphasizes explicit domains, assumptions, transformations, invariants, compatibility conditions, provenance, and open proof obligations.

This repository publishes a **bounded, non-sensitive overview** intended to invite independent scientific scrutiny. The included elementary algebra and metric-space examples are classical mathematical statements, not claims of original proof.

The research directions include three major open problems: the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem, the Riemann Hypothesis, and the Hodge Conjecture. **This repository makes no claim that any of these problems has been solved.**

Internal computational materials, original unpublished manuscripts, personal data, and sensitive implementation details are **not part of the public release**. Restricted materials may be considered for supervised evaluation only under separate explicit authorization.
